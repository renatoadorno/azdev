# Team conventions — <team or project>

Project `<project>` at `<orgUrl>`. What `flows.json` cannot express: rules, roles and traps that change how a card is created, named or closed. Updated on <date>.

## Writing

- Format of descriptions and comments (Markdown or HTML), and whether `richTextFormat` is set.
- How to reference work items (`#id` autolinks) and pull requests (full URL).
- How to mention someone in a comment.

## Types and states

- Types whose states differ from the default, listed with their states.
- Types with no `Removed`, and how the team discards them (for example "close with Done and a comment saying why").

## Sprints

- The backlog sprint, if there is one, and what goes there.
- Iterations nobody looks at (an item sent there disappears).
- Where cards that outlive the story's sprint go (publication, follow-ups).

## Story cycle and roles

- Who usually reviews, tests and publishes, with emails.
- Cards of a story that sit with other people by design.
- States that mean "waiting", not "not done".

## Titles

- Markers that identify a card (`[PROD]`, `[HOMOLOG]`, …) and their variants in use.
- Titles that hide delivery and how to fix them.

## Quality rules and metrics

- Why the cards listed in `flows.json` matter (the list itself lives there), and what their description must contain.
- What happens when one is missing (reports, metrics).
- How to record a retest after a later change.
- What not to edit retroactively.

## Delivery analysis

- How the team reads delivery (by story, not by assignee).
- Operational work kept apart.
